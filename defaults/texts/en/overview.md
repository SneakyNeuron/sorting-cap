# Sorting Cap

I am a hat. I learn who you are, go through the job listings you point me to, and keep only the ones worth your time. For the listings you approve, I research the company and draft a motivation letter that has survived a sceptic and a fact-check.

- Everything runs in your terminal. Your data stays on your machine unless you pick a remote provider yourself.
- Everything I know about you lives in plain files in your workspace. You can read and edit them.
- I propose, you decide. Nothing is sent on its own.
